I've been having bad cramps - is that normal?

What is Bloom?

Bloom combines period and symptom tracking with guided health education. It supports menstruation, fertility awareness, and menopause/perimenopause symptom tracking for Jamaican communities.

  • Track your period, flow intensity, and symptoms day by day
  • View your cycle on an interactive calendar
  • Chat with Bloomie, our guided health assistant
  • Read educational pamphlets written for Jamaican audiences
  • Find reproductive health clinics across Jamaica
  • Choose to stay anonymous or create an account for cloud sync
