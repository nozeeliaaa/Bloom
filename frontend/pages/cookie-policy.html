Cookie Policy

Effective as of February 21, 2026

Bloom is a Jamaican-developed reproductive health support application ("Bloom," "we," "our," or "us"). This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies on the Bloom website and web application.

This Policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your web browser when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, remember your preferences, and improve user experience.

Similar technologies may also be used, such as local storage, session storage, and security tokens. For simplicity, this Policy refers to all such technologies as "cookies."

Cookies may store limited information such as:

  • Session identifiers
  • Login status
  • Device or browser type
  • Consent preferences

Bloom does not use cookies to store or process reproductive health logs.

Types of Cookies We Use

1. Necessary Cookies (Required)

These cookies are essential for the website or web application to function properly. They allow you to:

  • Log into secure areas
  • Maintain session state
  • Remember cookie consent preferences
  • Protect against fraudulent or automated access

Because these cookies are strictly necessary for core functionality, they cannot be disabled through our cookie banner.

Examples may include session cookies for authentication, security cookies used to prevent bot traffic, and cookie consent preference storage. Session cookies expire when you close your browser. Persistent necessary cookies may remain for a limited period (typically up to 12 months).

2. Performance & Basic Analytics Cookies (If Enabled)

Bloom may use limited analytics tools to understand how users interact with the website in order to improve navigation, identify technical errors, monitor system performance, and understand general usage trends.

  • These cookies do not collect reproductive health data.
  • They collect aggregated, non-sensitive usage information.
  • Where required by applicable law, these cookies are activated only after you provide consent through our cookie banner.

What We Do Not Use

  • Advertising cookies
  • Behavioural marketing trackers
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • Cross-site profiling technologies
  • Cookies to analyse reproductive health logs

We do not sell cookie data to third parties.

First-Party vs Third-Party Cookies

First-party cookies are set directly by Bloom. Third-party cookies may be set by infrastructure providers such as Google Firebase or security providers operating on our behalf. Any third-party cookies used operate strictly under contractual safeguards and in accordance with the Jamaica Data Protection Act, 2020.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

When you visit Bloom, you may see a cookie banner that allows you to accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or customise your preferences. You can change your preferences at any time through the cookie settings option on the website.

You may also manage or delete cookies directly through your browser settings. Please note that disabling necessary cookies may affect certain features, such as login functionality.

Data Protection and Cookies

Cookies are processed in accordance with the Jamaica Data Protection Act, 2020 (DPA 2020). Bloom processes cookie-related data:

  • Lawfully and fairly
  • For specified and legitimate purposes
  • Using data minimisation principles
  • With appropriate technical and organisational safeguards

Cookie data is not used to make automated medical decisions and does not include sensitive health logs.

Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or system features. The "Effective Date" at the top of this Policy will indicate when it was last revised.
