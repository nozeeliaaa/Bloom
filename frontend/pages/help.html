Step-by-step guides for the most common actions in Bloom.

Log a period day

  1. 1Open Calendar from the navigation bar.
  2. 2Tap or click the day you want to log.
  3. 3In the day panel, select your flow intensity (light, medium, heavy, or spotting).
  4. 4Optionally add symptoms, a mood, and a note.
  5. 5Press Save. The day will be coloured on the calendar and your cycle stats will update.

Switch your health goal

  1. 1Open Settings from your profile page.
  2. 2Find the Health Goal section and select your goal (Track my cycle, Get pregnant, I am pregnant, or Manage symptoms).
  3. 3Save. The Dashboard will immediately adapt its cards and insights to your chosen goal.

Read the Dashboard

  1. 1The top card shows your current cycle day and estimated phase (menstrual, follicular, ovulation, luteal).
  2. 2Below that, goal-specific cards appear - for example, a fertile window for TTC users or a due date for pregnancy.
  3. 3The Cycle History section shows a chart and table of your past cycles. Use Export as PDF to download a report.
  4. 4Advanced Insights (account mode only) flags any unusual patterns Bloom has noticed.

Use Bloomie (chat assistant)

  1. 1Tap Bloomie in the nav or the pink chat button on any page.
  2. 2Type a question or choose a quick-reply button to start.
  3. 3Bloomie gives educational responses. It is not a doctor - for medical concerns, always consult a healthcare provider.

Switch between Anonymous and Account mode

  1. 1Anonymous mode stores all data on your device only. Good for privacy, but data is lost if you clear your browser.
  2. 2Account mode backs data up securely to the cloud and unlocks advanced features. You must be 18+.
  3. 3To upgrade from anonymous, go to Profile and tap Create Account.

Common questions about Bloom.

How do I log my period?+
Go to Calendar, tap a day, select your flow intensity (light, medium, heavy, or spotting), optionally add symptoms, then press Save.
How is my cycle length calculated?+
Bloom detects your period start days from your flow logs, then counts the days between consecutive starts. You need at least two period starts before a cycle length can be calculated.
Is my data private?+
In Anonymous mode your data never leaves your device - it is stored in your browser's local storage. In Account mode data is encrypted and stored securely. We never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
How do I download a health report?+
From the Dashboard, press Export as PDF in the Cycle History section. The report downloads immediately without leaving the page.
What do the cycle phases mean?+
Menstrual - bleeding days. Follicular - after your period ends, leading up to ovulation. Ovulation - estimated peak fertile day. Luteal - after ovulation until your next period. See the Glossary tab for full definitions.
Can Bloom predict my next period?+
Yes - once you have logged at least two cycles, Bloom estimates your next period start based on your average cycle length. The more cycles you log, the more accurate the prediction. Predictions are educational estimates only.
What if I have irregular cycles?+
Bloom will flag cycle variability in Advanced Insights and give lower-confidence predictions. Keep logging - even irregular data builds a useful record over time. If irregularity is persistent or concerning, speak with a healthcare provider.
How do I switch my health goal?+
Go to Profile → Settings → Health Goal and select a new goal. The Dashboard adapts immediately.
Why did my cycle data disappear?+
In Anonymous mode, data is stored in your browser. Clearing your browser history or cache can erase it. To protect your data, create a free account to enable cloud backup.
Is Bloom a substitute for medical advice?+
No. Bloom is an educational tool only. It does not diagnose conditions or replace a doctor. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical concerns.
