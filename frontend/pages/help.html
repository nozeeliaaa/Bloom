FAQs

How do I log my period?+
Go to Calendar, tap a day, choose your flow, symptoms, then save.
How is my cycle length calculated?+
Bloom detects your period start days from your flow logs, then measures the days between starts.
Is my data private?+
Your logs stay on your device in this demo (localStorage). In a full version, we’d add secure storage + consent controls.
How do I download a report?+
Open Report for Doctor and click Download Health Report.
