Accessibility Statement

Prepared: February 21, 2026

This is the accessibility statement for the following digital products operated by Bloom ("we," "us," or "our"):

  • Bloom web application
  • Bloom website

Our Commitment to Accessibility

At Bloom, we believe reproductive health information should be accessible to everyone. We are committed to inclusion, usability, and reducing barriers for users with disabilities.

Accessibility is considered during the design and development of our digital products, and we strive to build features that are usable by individuals with diverse abilities and assistive technologies.

We aim to:

  • Design with accessibility in mind during development
  • Use clear language and readable content
  • Support keyboard navigation where applicable
  • Provide sufficient color contrast for readability
  • Ensure form fields and interactive elements are labeled appropriately
  • Test new features for usability before release

As a developing digital product, accessibility is an ongoing process, and improvements are made as updates are implemented.

Conformance Status

Bloom aims to align with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA, which is an internationally recognized accessibility standard.

At this time, Bloom is partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

Partially conformant means that while many areas meet accessibility standards, some features may not yet fully satisfy all technical requirements. We are actively working to improve accessibility as the platform evolves.

Accessibility Features

Bloom may include accessibility-supportive features such as:

  • Scalable text compatibility with browser zoom settings
  • Compatibility with common screen readers
  • Logical navigation structure
  • Clear form validation messages
  • Descriptive button labels
  • Reduced reliance on color alone to convey meaning

Accessibility improvements are prioritized in ongoing updates.

Assessment Approach

The accessibility of Bloom’s digital products has been assessed through internal review and self-evaluation during development and testing.

As Bloom evolves, we may conduct additional reviews to improve accessibility compliance and user experience.

Feedback and Reporting Accessibility Issues

We welcome feedback on the accessibility of Bloom. If you experience difficulty accessing any part of our digital products or require assistance, please contact us:

Bloom
Email: Bloomhelpdesk@outlook.com

When reporting an issue, please include:

  • The page or feature where you experienced difficulty
  • The device and browser you were using
  • A brief description of the issue

We aim to respond within a reasonable timeframe and will make reasonable efforts to address accessibility concerns.

Ongoing Commitment

Bloom is a developing digital health platform. Accessibility is a continuous effort, and we are committed to improving usability and inclusivity over time.
