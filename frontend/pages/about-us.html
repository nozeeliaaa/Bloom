Who we are

Bloom was developed by a team of student innovators who are passionate about using technology to create meaningful, people-centred solutions. As Computer Science students, we recognised the need for a digital platform that could support reproductive health awareness in a way that is informative, accessible, and relevant to the lives of users, particularly within the Caribbean context.

Our team created Bloom with the goal of combining thoughtful design, practical functionality, and educational value into one unified platform. We wanted to build more than just a tracking tool. We wanted to create a supportive digital environment where users could learn about their cycles, access health resources, and feel more empowered in understanding their reproductive wellness.

The development of Bloom reflects our commitment to solving real-world problems through technology. We approached this project with a strong focus on user experience, accessibility, and ethical design. Every feature within Bloom was developed with care to ensure that the platform remains clear, supportive, and easy to use while still addressing an important area of personal health.
