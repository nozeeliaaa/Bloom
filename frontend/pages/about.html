About Bloom

Bloom supports cycle awareness for Jamaican users through simple tracking and educational insights.

Our Mission

Bloom empowers individuals to understand their cycle, track patterns, and make informed health decisions in a supportive and private environment.

Our Values

  • Privacy & Security - you control your data.
  • Education & Transparency - clear explanations, no fear tactics.
  • Empowerment through data - patterns that help you advocate for yourself.
  • Inclusive design - accessible, culturally aware, easy to use.
